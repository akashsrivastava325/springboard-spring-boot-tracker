One path. Three ways to study.

Learning path: follow all 204 questions in order. Check a question when you can explain it in your own words.

Complete notes: your full explanation, examples, code, and dry runs. This view only shows items with Complete notes.

Skim notes: short rules, gotchas, and instant-recall hints. This view only shows items with Skim notes. Both views follow your original question order and share the same filters.

Use the red “+ Pre-REQUISITE” button below a question’s notes button to insert a personal question or standalone note immediately before that question. Use “Add your own” to append an item to a section. Edit the title or delete an addition from its note editor. Deleted additions can be undone immediately.

Ask: what problem exists → how it works → one tiny example → what changes if I remove it. Use a Student Management application as your running example.

Your data stays with you.

Guest notes save in this browser on this device. Sign in to save a private notebook in Firebase and access it using the same account on your phone or computer. Wait for Saved to your account before switching devices. Use Copy guest notebook to account to move existing notes after confirmation. Keep an Export backup too. Import replaces the current notebook, including its cloud copy when signed in, only after you confirm.

If you move from a preview or downloaded file to the published site, use export/import to move your data to the new address. Clearing browser/site data removes guest notes and unsynced local changes. Signed-in notes are sent to your private Firebase notebook; local account caches remain in this browser after sign-out. If two devices change the notebook, sync pauses so you can choose which copy to keep. Cloud notebooks support up to 900 KB of data.
